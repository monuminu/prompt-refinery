# GPT-6 prompt revision playbook

## Initiative
Define the finished deliverable. For action tasks, specify how far to proceed, which routine assumptions are acceptable, and which missing decisions require a question. Prepare reviewable work before an approval boundary.

## Instruction clarity
Resolve contradictions. Distinguish requirements from preferences and supporting material. If skills or instruction files are relevant, explain their priority and require a specific explanation when they block progress.

## Writing
Specify audience, length, tone and format. Favor concrete language and useful structure. Remove repetitive framing and stock phrases when they do not serve the task.

## Delegation
If the environment actually supports subagents and parallel work helps, define when to delegate and what each agent should return. Do not invent delegation capabilities.

## Verification
Match checks to the work's impact. Define evidence of success and a stopping condition. Avoid redundant checks after sufficient verification. Evaluate revised prompts on representative tasks before adopting them.
